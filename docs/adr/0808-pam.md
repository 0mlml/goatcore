# ADR 0808 — PAM — Kanidm + sudo audit + session recording + JIT elevation

## TL;DR (for a layperson)

Privileged access management (PAM) covers how operators get
admin (root / sudo) access. Our pattern: identities come from
Kanidm, every sudo call is audited, sessions are recorded
when elevated, and elevation is just-in-time — operators
don't have standing root, they request and receive root only
for the duration they need it.

## Context

Standing root access is one of the largest insider-risk
surfaces. Even with sudo, audit logs are easy to lose, and
session content (what commands actually ran) is rarely
captured.

The PAM pattern: pair sudo with continuous audit, record
sessions during elevated operations, and require explicit
elevation per action.

## Decision

PAM covers four properties:

- **Identity from Kanidm** ([`0402`](0402-kanidm-for-accounts.md)).
  No local accounts on mgmt-hosts beyond the seeded operator
  account.
- **Sudo audit**: every sudo call writes to auditd
  ([`0800`](0800-open-source-edr.md)) and ships to the
  audit-integrity tier.
- **Session recording**: elevated sessions are recorded
  (asciinema-format or similar) and stored to the audit-
  integrity tier with retention policy.
- **JIT elevation**: operators request elevation per task
  through the policy engine
  ([`0805`](0805-policy-engine-active-passive.md)) — fast
  path for routine ops, normal path for unusual.
- **MFA for elevation**: FIDO2
  ([`0403`](0403-fido2-break-glass.md)) required for sudo.

Tracked as Block 18B / 3D in
`../project/implementation-plan.md`.

## Alternatives considered

- **Standing root for operators.** Insider-risk surface.
  Rejected.
- **Sudo with file-based audit only.** Loses session content
  + ships unreliably. Rejected.
- **Commercial PAM product (Beyond Trust, etc.).** Closed-
  source, sovereignty issues. Rejected.

## Consequences

**Positive.** Standing root eliminated. Every elevation
audited. Session content preserved. MFA required.

**Negative.** Friction for routine ops. Mitigated by fast-
path JIT elevation through the policy engine.

## Assessor view (SCA-R)

PAM serves AC-2 (account management), AC-6 (least privilege),
IA-2 (multi-factor for privileged access), AU-2 (audit), and
AU-12 (audit generation). Session recording aligns with NIST
SP 800-53 AU-14 (session audit). The combination is
substantively stronger than median DoD PAM posture; see
`../compliance/comparative-posture.md`.

## Trigger conditions for revisit

- Operational friction from JIT elevation becomes
  unsustainable. Investigate routine-op automation; do not
  abandon JIT.
- A specific authorization mandates a specific PAM product.
  Negotiate; preserve the substantive properties.
