Permissions matrix

Who may reach which service, and what each service's front door decides. This page is rendered from the permissions matrix that the fences are checked against; when a survey of the fences and this page disagree, this page wins.

A dash is a denial, and only a group-gated column has dashes. Only a group-gated column is a complete account of who gets in. On every other column a cell the matrix grants nothing for says what the door admits instead: any login — you must be someone (any Ory identity, any group; the visit is recorded); app's own gate — no proxy, the application asks you to log in and decides for itself; no login — you need not be anyone: the only gate is the mesh IP allowlist, and nothing records who came. Columns marked ⚠ are not group-gated.
