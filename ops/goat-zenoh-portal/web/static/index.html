What this is

The __LEX_PRODUCT_NAME__ Portal is the front door for the fabric. Operators register __LEX_ORGS__ here and hand them a credential bundle; each __LEX_ORG__ then publishes messages onto Zenoh under its reserved namespace. Once data is flowing, the Register a topic page is where __LEX_ORGS__ declare what they're publishing (key pattern, format, schema reference), and the Inspector page is where anyone on the fabric can browse live values.

See the operator-facing namespace + ACL contract in the project's design doc set.
