What's on this page

Every stream is listed for everyone — seeing that a producer exists is free. Seeing its contents may need a grant. A stream's access label says which: community (anyone here), org (its own organization), or controlled (by grant only). Request access to a restricted producer below; the producer's custodian approves, minting an enumerable, expiry-able grant row. Restricting is deliberate (you say why); opening is effortless.

Producer catalog

Every registered producer and its effective access lane. Request access to a restricted one.

Requests inbox

Pending requests you can decide (your own producer's, or all as operator). Approving mints a grant.

Grants

Active + historical grant rows. Revoking end-dates a grant (the row survives for the audit trail).

Grant preemptively

Grant a producer's data to an org or a person before anyone asks (ADR 1075 §12). Org-scope is the coalition default.
