Echo Loopback

A round-trip ping for your publisher. You publish a payload on your own topic; the fabric echoes it back on a sibling topic; your subscriber confirms it received what you sent. Green echo = you're correctly on the fabric end-to-end: cert is valid, ACL accepts your prefix, your subscriber wildcard is right, the wire is carrying your bytes.

Preview — this surface isn't live yet on the trial fabric. We're sharing the planned shape here so you can wire your client against it ahead of time. See the “In the meantime” section below for how to confirm wire-reachability today.

How it will work

Both halves of the round-trip live in your own namespace:

  • You publish on <your-prefix>/system/echo/request/v1
  • The fabric echoes back on <your-prefix>/system/echo/response/v1
  • You subscribe on the response key and confirm round-trip

Payload is opaque bytes (up to 4 KiB); the response carries the same payload back plus a small {request_id, received_at} header in a JSON envelope so you can correlate.

Python example (against the bundle)

import zenoh
import time, uuid

cfg = zenoh.Config.from_file("path/to/your/zenoh.json5")
session = zenoh.open(cfg)

# Subscribe to the echo response FIRST so you don't miss it.
got = {}
def on_echo(sample):
    got["payload"] = bytes(sample.payload)
    got["key"] = str(sample.key_expr)

sub = session.declare_subscriber("my-__LEX_ORG__/system/echo/response/v1", on_echo)

# Publish a request with a unique correlation ID.
req_id = uuid.uuid4().hex
session.put("my-__LEX_ORG__/system/echo/request/v1", req_id.encode())

# Wait up to 5s for the echo.
for _ in range(50):
    if got:
        break
    time.sleep(0.1)

assert got, "no echo within 5s"
print("round-trip OK:", got["payload"].decode())

In the meantime

Three ways to confirm your bytes are actually on the wire today, without the echo surface:

Topology view
Open panoscope ↗ and search for your __LEX_ORG__ prefix. If your publisher is connected, your peer shows up as a node with edges to the topics it's publishing. No node = you're not on the fabric yet.
Inspector decode
Register your topic on the Register a topic page, upload a .fds schema body, then open the Inspector and pick your topic. If samples are flowing, you'll see them decoded in real time. If samples don't flow, the wire isn't carrying your bytes yet.
Connectivity probe
If you're not sure whether the problem is reachability vs. wire-format, run the Connectivity Probe first. It takes about 30 seconds and tells you which of DNS / TCP / TLS / cert chain / URI-SAN is broken.
