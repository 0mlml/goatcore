Config Workbench

Paste a zenohd.json5 draft on the left. The linter runs as you type and surfaces structured findings on the right against the trial fabric's contract: router mode, our endpoint, mTLS posture, all cert paths set, ACL rules covering your prefix, version pin.

Use this when you've hand-edited a generated config (or written one from scratch) and want a sanity check before standing the container up. For a fresh known-good template, hit Load template with your intended prefix — same content as Router Config, just dropped straight into the paste box.
