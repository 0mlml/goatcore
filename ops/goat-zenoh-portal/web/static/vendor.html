__LEX_ORGS_TITLE__

Who publishes on this fabric, what topics they own, and where to inspect each one.
How to read this page

One card per __LEX_ORG__ (a publisher identity; its 32-hex prefix is the write anchor). Each row is a registered topic. Click a row to open live samples in the Inspector; use the row's quiet buttons for the schema, a stethoscope diagnosis, or a flashlight tail. The dot in a card header shows the most recent publish recorded under that __LEX_ORG__ by the fabric audit log (batched per minute, so up to a minute behind): green under 5 min, amber under 24 h, grey older, hollow none recorded. The portal does not track publish times per topic, so none is shown per row. Keyboard: while the topic list has focus, / search, ↑↓ pick, Enter open, Esc clear, j/k move between rows.
